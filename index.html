Pg. 02 How I navigate

Four bearings I travel by.

Whether building a function from scratch or scaling one that already exists, the route stays the same: understand the terrain, design for the customer, automate the repeatable, and report back to base.

  1. N

    Map the problem with data

    KPI dashboards in Excel, Power BI and SQL, plus root-cause analysis on detractors, surface exactly where a journey is breaking down.

  2. E

    Design the experience

    CX strategy, NPS/CSAT programs, WhatsApp-led communication and self-serve help centres that cut friction before it becomes a ticket.

  3. S

    Automate the routine

    AI-powered workflows that took assignment evaluation from 5 days to 12 hours: automation in service of the customer, not around them.

  4. W

    Report back to base

    Quarterly business reviews to CXOs and KPI dashboards across 12 centres and 8 cities, so leadership always knows where things stand.

Bengaluru, India

Apr 2026 – Present

Manager – Customer Experience, Founder's Office

  • Own customer experience and retention for a 20+ course business with a ~₹3 Cr monthly sales target; lead a 12-member team managing 8,500+ customer interactions per month.
  • Designed and run a refund-retention program, retaining ~80% of 80+ monthly refund requests and protecting ~₹19L/month.
